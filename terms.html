1. What Iqra is

Iqra is a learning tool for reading, reciting and memorizing the Quran. It includes lessons, quizzes, games, recitation grading, a chat coach (Nur), prayer times and other helpers.

2. Not a substitute for a teacher or scholar

Recitation grading and Nur use AI. They can make mistakes, miss subtle tajweed points (such as exact madd length) and misunderstand your speech. They are practice partners, not teachers.

Nur can answer general Islamic questions, but it is not a scholar and does not issue fatwas. For rulings that affect your own situation, ask a qualified scholar. Prayer times are calculated estimates; confirm with your local mosque if exact timing matters. Quran text and translations come from third-party sources; for study of the text itself, consult a printed mushaf and a qualified teacher.
