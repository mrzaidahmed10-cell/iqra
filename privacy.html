Privacy Policy

Effective October 18, 2026 · Last updated October 18, 2026

Iqra is a Quran-learning app. This page explains, in plain language, what we collect, why, who else sees it, and how you can export or delete it. We do not run ads, we do not sell your data, and we do not use analytics or tracking cookies.

1. What we collect

DataWhyWhere it lives
Account: username, email, passwordSign in and sync across devicesOur database (Cloudflare KV). Passwords are salted and hashed (PBKDF2); we cannot read them.
Profile: name, age, gender (optional), level, daily goal, placement quiz resultPersonalize lessons and coach strictnessYour device, and our database if you have an account
Progress: XP, coins, streak, lessons, learned ayahs, badges, settings, recitation scores, Nur chat historySave and sync your learningYour device, and our database if you have an account
Voice recordings (only when you tap record)Transcribe and grade your recitationSent to our AI provider for processing. Iqra does not store the audio. The copy kept for replay lives only in your browser's memory and disappears when you close the recitation or the page.

Location (only if you choose "Use my location" for prayer times)Show prayer timesKept on your device. The coordinates (or the city you type) are sent to the prayer-times service to get the times.
Technical data such as IP address and request logsSecurity, abuse prevention, rate limitsProcessed by our hosting provider (Cloudflare). We keep short-lived counters, for example failed sign-ins and daily AI-use limits, which expire automatically.

2. Who else processes your data

  • Cloudflare hosts the site and our database.
  • Groq processes recordings (speech to text) and chat/coaching text for the AI features. What Groq does with data is governed by its own terms and privacy policy.
  • alquran.cloud, Islamic Network, EveryAyah and Quran.com supply Quran text, translations, word-by-word meanings and recitation audio. Your browser contacts them directly when you read or listen, so they can see your IP address and which content you request.
  • Aladhan supplies prayer times.

We do not sell or rent personal data and we do not share it for advertising.

3. Cookies and storage

Iqra sets no cookies. It uses your browser's local storage and IndexedDB to keep your progress, settings, sign-in token and cached Quran data on your device. Clearing site data or signing out removes them from that device.

4. AI features and accuracy

Recitation grading and Nur are produced by AI. They can be wrong. Please do not enter anything you would not want processed by an AI provider. Recordings should contain recitation, not private conversation.

5. How long we keep data

  • Account and progress data: until you delete your account.
  • Sign-in sessions: expire after 60 days.
  • Rate-limit counters: expire within about two days.
  • Voice recordings: not retained by Iqra.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Access and portability: Account → Export my progress downloads your data as a file.
  • Correction: edit your profile and sign-in details in Account.
  • Deletion: Account → Delete account permanently removes your account and synced progress. You can also clear your Nur chat at any time.
  • Withdraw features: you can use Iqra without recording or chatting.

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California) you may have additional rights, such as objecting to processing or complaining to a regulator. Contact us using the details below and we will respond.

7. Security

Traffic is encrypted with HTTPS, passwords are hashed, sessions expire, and sign-in attempts are rate limited. No online service is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

8. Children and families

Iqra is open to learners of every age. We ask only for the information needed to run the app and do not use it for advertising. Parents and guardians should supervise younger children, set up their account and help them decide whether to use the recording and chat features. If you want a child's data exported or deleted, use the in-app options or contact us and we will help.

9. International use

Our providers may process data in the United States and other countries. By using Iqra you understand your data may be transferred there.

10. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
